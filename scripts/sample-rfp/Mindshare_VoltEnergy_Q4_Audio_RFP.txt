MINDSHARE USA — AUDIO REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
Client: Volt Energy Drinks
Campaign: "Charge the Season" Q4 Holiday Push
Issued: September 28, 2026
Proposals due: Friday, October 9, 2026 by 5:00 PM ET

1. CONTACTS
Media Planner: Priya Shah (priya.shah@mindshareworld.com)
Associate Media Director: Marcus Lee
Please submit proposals to both contacts.

2. CAMPAIGN OVERVIEW
Volt Energy Drinks is launching a limited-edition holiday flavor and needs to drive
trial through a trackable promo code (VOLTCHARGE) redeemable on volt.com and at
participating retailers. Success will be measured primarily on promo code
redemptions; secondary KPI is lift in aided brand awareness.

3. FLIGHT
Flight dates: October 19, 2026 – December 27, 2026 (10 weeks)
Heavy-up weeks: Thanksgiving week and the two weeks before Christmas.

4. BUDGET
Total gross budget: $450,000 (inclusive of all production and talent fees)
Target CPM: $22.00 net or better for streaming; podcast host-read packages will be
evaluated on a flat-rate basis.
Impression goal: 18MM total impressions across all channels.

5. AUDIENCE
Primary: Adults 21-44, skew male 60/40
Secondary: College students and young professionals, HHI $50K+
Interest targeting: sports fans, gamers, fitness enthusiasts, comedy listeners.

6. CHANNELS & FORMATS
- Podcast: host-read mid-rolls in sports and comedy shows (please include talent options)
- Streaming audio: :30 spots with companion display banners
- Satellite radio: live reads on sports talk channels during NFL and college football coverage
Video and live events are out of scope for this RFP.

7. CREATIVE & RESTRICTIONS
- No placement adjacent to political, news, or true crime content.
- Host-read copy must be approved by client legal 5 business days before air.
- Talent may not mention competitor brands (Red Bull, Monster, Celsius).

8. DELIVERABLES
- Media plan by channel with weekly flighting
- Added-value proposal (bonus impressions, social amplification)
- Post-campaign report with promo code attribution within 30 days of flight end
